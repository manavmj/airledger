      OPEN METHODOLOGY

      Evidence before eloquence.

      What the analysis does

      Normalizes supported mass units to µg/m³. Excludes missing or invalid readings, unknown units, duplicate rows, and conflicting values at the same location, time, pollutant and averaging period. Flags robust statistical outliers (MAD, at least five observations) while retaining them. Does not fill missing data.

      What GenAI does

      Gemini suggests column mappings for your review, then selects verified finding IDs and audience-appropriate action IDs. The server rejects unknown IDs. A controlled multilingual renderer supplies the wording and computed values, so the model cannot insert fabricated measurements. Selection can still be imperfect and needs human review.

      What the brief can’t say

      No official AQI, live conditions, causes, health outcomes, or forecasts are inferred. WHO 2021 daily comparisons require explicitly stated 24-hour averages. Monitor accuracy is not verified.

      Language & reading level

      English, Hindi and Marathi have controlled advisory templates with easy and standard reading variants. These are not certified translations or measured reading-grade scores. Local review is required before public distribution.

      Your data

      Uploads and briefs stay on this server, scoped to an opaque browser session, for up to 24 hours. In AI mode, column labels or computed facts with location labels are sent to Google Gemini. Do not upload personal or confidential data. Clearing the workspace deletes its database records; this is not forensic disk erasure.
